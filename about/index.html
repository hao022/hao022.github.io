About

HUATUO is an open source technology community focused on foundational software. Its work covers the Linux kernel, cloud native technologies, AI infrastructure, and Agent sandboxes. The community advances innovation and practical adoption in system-level observability, performance analysis, and fault diagnosis. We welcome contributions from developers, companies, and ecosystem partners. Financial contributions and Token resources are also welcome to support the community’s continued development.
